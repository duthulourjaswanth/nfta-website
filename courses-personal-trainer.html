Certified Personal Trainer

This isn't a course that teaches you to count reps. NFTA's personal trainer certification trains you to assess a client, design a program around their actual limitations, and adjust it when the plan meets reality.

Enroll Now

Why Train With NFTA

Most personal trainer courses teach you to follow a template. NFTA teaches you to build one — from movement assessment through progressive programming — so you're prepared for the client in front of you, not just the client in the textbook.

What You'll Learn

  • Anatomy, physiology & movement mechanics — how the body moves and adapts under load
  • Program design & periodization — plans that progress logically, not by guesswork
  • Client assessment & correction — spot movement faults before they become injuries
  • Communication & coaching skills — motivate and guide clients toward results they'll stick with
  • Gym & business fundamentals — run sessions, manage clients, operate professionally

Who This Course Is For

  • Career switchers entering the fitness industry
  • Gym staff formalizing their training knowledge
  • Enthusiasts turning passion into a profession
  • Anyone who tried generic online certs and wants one that's applied
