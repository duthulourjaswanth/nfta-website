About NFTA

Built on a simple idea: certification should mean you can actually coach — not just that you passed a test.

Our Story

NFTA was founded in 2023 by Saravanan "Sharu" Hariram — a trainer, sports nutritionist, and competitive bodybuilder who saw a gap in Indian fitness education: too many certifications taught theory disconnected from what coaching a real client actually requires.

NFTA was built to close that gap — combining scientific grounding with the applied, hands-on skill that coaching demands. The journey started earlier, in 2007, when Sharu began certifying trainers in South India; since then he has empowered 2,500+ trainers to build real careers in a fast-evolving industry.

About the Founder & Trainer

"Sharu" Saravanan Hariram

Founder & Chief Training Officer · Sports Nutritionist · International Bodybuilder

In 2007, a certification course for trainers was initiated in South India by International Master Trainer and Sports Nutritionist "Sharu" Saravanan Hariram — with an objective to give students a solid understanding of the scientific principles related to fitness. He strongly believed that incorporating scientific knowledge into training programs is crucial for their effectiveness, because a structured educational program enables individuals to responsibly pursue their fitness and science-integrated goals.

So far, through his Certified Fitness Trainer course, he has empowered more than 2,500 trainers and fitness enthusiasts to excel in the ever-evolving fitness industry — competent professionals who demonstrate their grasp of both the theoretical and practical aspects of fitness.
