Weight Loss Nutritionist

Weight loss advice is everywhere, and most of it doesn't hold up past week two. This certification trains you to build sustainable, evidence-based strategies — and coach clients through the part that actually determines results: staying consistent.

Enroll Now

Why Get Certified Here

Most weight-loss advice fails not because the science is wrong, but because the plan doesn't survive real life. This course trains you to build weight-management strategies that account for that — and to coach clients through the plateaus and setbacks that derail generic diet plans.

01

Evidence-Based, Not Fad-Based

Learn the actual physiology of sustainable weight loss, not the latest trend.

02

Built for Real Behavior

Coach through low-adherence weeks, plateaus, and the psychology of weight loss — not just the meal plan.

03

Learn Your Way

Online, offline, or hybrid formats to fit your schedule.

What You'll Learn

  • Metabolic fundamentals & weight-management science — energy balance, metabolic adaptation, what drives fat loss
  • Meal planning for weight loss — realistic, individualized strategies clients can stick to
  • Client assessment & goal setting — targets that are ambitious but achievable
  • Coaching psychology & behavior change — why clients fall off track and how to coach them back
  • Plateau management — recognize and adjust when progress stalls

Who This Course Is For

  • Personal trainers adding weight-loss coaching to their offering
  • Career switchers interested in nutrition & weight management
  • Enthusiasts formalizing their knowledge into a certified skill
  • Anyone who wants to understand — and teach — what actually works
